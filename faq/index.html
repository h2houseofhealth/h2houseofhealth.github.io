1. Products & Molecular Hydrogen
What is Molecular Hydrogen (H₂) and how does it work?

Molecular hydrogen is the smallest and most bioavailable antioxidant in the universe. Because of its microscopic size, H₂ rapidly diffuses across cell membranes and the blood-brain barrier to selectively neutralize toxic hydroxyl free radicals (*OH) while preserving beneficial signaling molecules, supporting cellular energy, recovery, and mitochondrial health.

How do I use the H2 Molecular Hydrogen Water Bottle?

Fill the bottle with filtered, mineral, or drinking water (avoid hot or carbonated liquids). Press the power button to initiate the electrolysis cycle. The platinum-titanium SPE/PEM electrolytic cell infuses the water with pure dissolved molecular hydrogen gas (up to 3000+ ppb) without generating harmful ozone or chlorine byproducts. Drink immediately for maximum bioavailability.

How does the H2 Hydrogen Mist Spray benefit skin?

The H2 Hydrogen Mist Spray uses high-frequency ultrasonic atomization combined with electrolysis to generate micro-fine hydrogen-rich water droplets. These droplets penetrate the dermal barrier to calm inflammation, fight oxidative environmental stress, promote deep hydration, and enhance natural skin barrier vitality.
