Security

Effective Date: July 2026

Last Updated: July 2026

At H2 House of Health, protecting your personal information is one of our highest priorities. We implement appropriate administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to help protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

How We Protect Your Information

  • Secure HTTPS encryption across our website.
  • Protected customer accounts and authentication.
  • Secure payment processing through trusted payment providers.
  • Restricted access to customer information.
  • Regular monitoring for suspicious activity.
  • Routine software updates and security improvements.

Your Responsibilities

To help keep your account secure, we recommend that you:

  • Create a strong password.
  • Do not share your login credentials.
  • Log out from shared or public devices.
  • Notify us immediately if you suspect unauthorized account access.

Payment Security

Payments are processed through trusted third-party payment providers. H2 House of Health does not store your complete payment card information on its servers.

Reporting Security Concerns

If you discover a security vulnerability or believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately.
