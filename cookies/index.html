What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit a website. They help websites recognize your browser, remember your preferences, improve performance, and provide a more personalized browsing experience.

Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Keep you signed in to your account.
  • Remember your preferences and website settings.
  • Support appointment booking and membership services.
  • Enable shopping cart functionality for merchandise purchases.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns.
  • Enhance website security and help prevent fraudulent activity.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are required for the proper operation of our website. They enable core functionality such as secure login, account management, appointment booking, shopping cart functionality, and access to protected areas.

Performance Cookies

Performance cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous usage information. This data helps us improve website performance and user experience.

Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences, such as language settings and previous choices, allowing us to provide a more personalized browsing experience.

Analytics Cookies

We may use trusted analytics services to better understand website usage, identify areas for improvement, and enhance our services. Analytics data is generally collected in an aggregated and non-identifiable manner.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by trusted third-party service providers that support payment processing, website analytics, cloud hosting, embedded content, or other website functionality. These providers maintain their own privacy and cookie policies.

Managing Your Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control, block, or delete cookies through your browser settings. You may choose to disable cookies at any time.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality, including account login, appointment booking, shopping cart features, and other personalized services.
