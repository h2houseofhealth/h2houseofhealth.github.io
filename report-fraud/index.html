Report Fraud

Effective Date: July 2026

Last Updated: July 2026

H2 House of Health is committed to protecting our customers from fraud and unauthorized activity. If you believe you have encountered fraudulent communications, suspicious transactions, or misuse of our services, please report it to us immediately.

Examples of Fraud

  • Emails pretending to be from H2 House of Health.
  • Fake payment requests.
  • Unauthorized account access.
  • Identity theft or impersonation.
  • Suspicious transactions or purchases.

How to Report Fraud

Please include the following information whenever possible:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • A description of the incident.
  • Relevant dates and times.
  • Screenshots or supporting evidence.

Our Response

Every report is reviewed by our team. If additional information is required, we may contact you to assist with the investigation.
